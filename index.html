Cleaning Methods

Laser Cleaning vs. Dry Ice Blasting vs. Traditional Methods

Each method has a different purpose. We help choose the right process based on the material, coating, contamination, and finish required.

Laser Cleaning

Best for precision rust, paint, oxide, and coating removal on metal. It is non-contact, controlled, and useful when the base surface needs to be preserved.

Dry Ice Blasting

Best for grease, soot, adhesives, residue, and general industrial grime. Dry ice turns to gas on impact, so it does not leave sand, water, or blasting media behind.

Sandblasting

Effective for aggressive stripping, but it can remove material, create dust, and leave abrasive media that needs cleanup.

Chemical Cleaning

Useful in some cases, but it can involve harsh products, disposal requirements, longer downtime, and extra surface rinsing or neutralizing.
