Dry Ice Cleaning

Clean Surfaces Without Water, Sand, or Harsh Chemical Runoff

Dry ice blasting uses compressed air and solid CO2 pellets to lift contamination from the surface. The dry ice turns into gas on impact, so there is no sand, water, or blasting media left behind.

No Secondary Media

The dry ice disappears as gas, leaving only the removed contamination to collect.

Dry Process

Useful where pressure washing would create water runoff, drying time, or extra cleanup.

Mobile Service

Cleaning can often be done on-site for equipment, concrete, machinery, and parts.

Comparison

Dry Ice Blasting vs. Sandblasting and Pressure Washing

Each cleaning method has a place. Dry ice blasting is strongest when the job needs less mess, less moisture, and no leftover abrasive media.

Dry Ice Blasting

Best for grease, soot, residue, grime, and buildup when a dry process and reduced cleanup are important.

Sandblasting

Useful for aggressive stripping, but it can abrade the surface and leave blasting media to clean up.

Pressure Washing

Useful for many exterior jobs, but it creates water runoff and drying time that may not fit every site.

Laser Cleaning

Useful for precise rust, oxide, paint, and coating removal on metal where controlled surface impact matters.

FAQ

Dry Ice Blasting Questions

Do you provide dry ice blasting in Vancouver?

Yes. Vanguard Blasting Solutions Ltd provides mobile dry ice blasting in Vancouver and nearby Lower Mainland communities by request.

Does dry ice blasting leave residue?

The dry ice turns into gas on impact, so it does not leave blasting media behind. The removed material still needs to be collected from the work area.

Is dry ice blasting safe for concrete?

It can be useful for many concrete cleaning jobs, especially surface buildup and deposits. A small test area is recommended before full cleaning.
